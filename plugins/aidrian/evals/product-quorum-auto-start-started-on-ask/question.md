# When a customer cancels within 24 hours of a slot, what happens to the slot?

## Context

A customer cancels a repair booking within 24 hours of its slot. The booking doc does not say whether the slot goes back on the calendar.

## Options

### A: Release at once

The slot goes back on the calendar the moment the customer cancels.

Rule changed: none

### B: Hold for the desk

The slot stays taken until the front desk releases it.

Rule changed: none
