# Front desk clerk

## Who

Works the counter of a bike shop and takes phone calls. Comfortable with the shop's till and calendar, and no more than that.

## Goals

Keep the day's repair calendar full, with no double bookings.

## Tasks

### Fill a free slot

Success: a walk-in customer's repair sits on today's calendar within a minute.

## Cares about

Knowing which slots are free right now, and not turning away a walk-in.

## Does not care about

How the calendar is stored, or how bookings reach it.

## Context

A desktop at the counter, in English, all day, interrupted often.
