# Sound credits

The four sounds are CC0 1.0 samples from Freesound pack 44741 (https://freesound.org), dedicated to the public domain:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. CC0 asks for no credit; this file says where they came from.

- `done.wav`: a turn is done.
- `attention.wav`: an approval or a question waits.
- `error.wav`: a turn or a command failed.
- `limit.wav`: a rate limit or a compaction.
