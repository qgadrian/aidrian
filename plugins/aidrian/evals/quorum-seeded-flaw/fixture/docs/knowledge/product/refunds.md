# Refunds

How Corner Shop gives money back. Any change to these rules needs the product owner.

## Critical flows

- **Refunds**: every path that gives a customer money back, from support or from the customer.
  A change to this flow goes through product review before anyone builds it.

## Store credit stays credit

A refund returns each part of an order the way the customer paid for it:
the card part goes back to the card, and the store-credit part goes back as store credit.
Store credit is never paid out to a card or as cash, even when the whole order is refunded.

## Refund window

A customer can refund an order for 30 days after it was placed. Support can refund later.
